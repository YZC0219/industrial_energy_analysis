Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **“Rolling Validation and Explainable Forecasting of Industrial Energy Use”** for possible publication in the *International Energy Journal*.

The manuscript reports a next-day industrial energy forecasting workflow evaluated on a fixed-seed synthetic workshop panel and, separately, on public steel-facility electricity data from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. It compares forecasting models under rolling-origin validation and reports feature ablations, fold-level statistical comparisons, and SHAP explanations. The manuscript distinguishes simulated results from the single-facility public-data analysis and does not claim causal effects or cross-facility validation.
